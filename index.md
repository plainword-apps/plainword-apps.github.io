# Plainword Apps

Small, focused tools for Atlassian products that let teams work in the formats they already use. Our apps run
entirely on Atlassian's own platform, and your data never leaves Atlassian.

## Markdown for Jira

Edit Jira issue descriptions in Markdown, with a live preview of how Jira will show them. Paste answers from ChatGPT
or Claude and keep their tables, lists, code and checklists.
