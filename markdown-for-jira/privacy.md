# Privacy policy: Markdown for Jira

_Plainword Apps · last updated 2 October 2026_

Markdown for Jira lets you edit a Jira issue's description in Markdown. This policy explains what happens to your
data when you use it.

## What the app does with your data

- When you open **Edit in Markdown**, the app reads that issue's description from Jira, as you, so Jira's own
  permissions apply.
- It converts the description to Markdown in your browser, and back again when you save.
- When you save, it writes the new description back to that issue, as you.

## What the app stores

Nothing. The app has no database or storage of its own, and keeps no copy of your descriptions, issues or any other
Jira data after the editor closes.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere outside Atlassian. The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian") and makes no
requests to any other server. Plainword Apps never receives your data.

## Analytics and cookies

The app collects no analytics, uses no tracking and sets no cookies.

## Personal data

The only personal data the app handles is what's already in a description you choose to edit (for example, a
mention of a colleague). It's passed between Jira and your browser and never stored or shared.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: use the support contact
on the app's Marketplace listing.
